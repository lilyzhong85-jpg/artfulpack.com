BUYING GUIDE

How to plan custom jewelry packaging

Choose your structure

Drawer boxes are the largest group in this range: a printed outer sleeve holds an inner tray that slides out to present rings, watches and other small pieces. Lid-and-base boxes and hinged specialty boxes suit a fuller boxed presentation, and magnetic gift boxes suit ribbon-tied gift sets. Pick the structure based on whether the piece is presented in a sliding tray or inside a full lid.

Insert and tray shaping

Drawer boxes and lid-and-base boxes can take an insert or shaped compartment cut for your items, so rings, watches or a multi-piece set sit securely rather than shifting inside the box. Tell us the item dimensions and we confirm the insert layout.

Print, finish and materials

Boxes in this range are made in corrugated board or coated art paper and printed by CMYK offset printing, with finishing options including matt or gloss lamination, spot UV, foil stamping and embossing/debossing depending on the structure.

Coordinate a jewelry collection

Several structures — drawer boxes, lid-and-base boxes and magnetic gift boxes — can share the same colour, ribbon and logo finish, so a set of rings, a watch box and an accessory box can present as one coordinated collection.

Sizing, quantity and lead time

Most styles in this range start at 500 or 1,000 pcs. Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and the design service is free.

QUESTIONS

Custom jewelry packaging FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom jewelry boxes?

Most styles in this range start at 500 or 1,000 pcs, and your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the structure and finish you choose.

Can the box include a tray or insert shaped for rings and watches?

Yes. Drawer boxes use a sliding inner tray, and lid-and-base boxes can take a paperboard or foam insert shaped to hold your specific pieces securely.

How long does production take, and do you ship internationally?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help design the jewelry packaging?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo and artwork references and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

Can I customize the ribbon, foil colour and logo placement?

Yes. Ribbon colour, foil-stamped or embossed logo finishes and insert layout are made to your brief on the structures that support them.

What are these jewelry boxes used for?

They're used for presenting rings, watches, necklaces and other accessory pieces at retail, for gifting, and for e-commerce shipping.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
