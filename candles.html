BUYING GUIDE

How to plan custom candle boxes

Choose your structure

Paper gift bags are the largest group in this range, suited to retail handover of a boxed or bagged candle. Magnetic and lid-and-base gift boxes with a shaped insert suit boxed candle-and-fragrance sets, a hexagonal specialty box adds a rope-handle presentation, and a paper tube with a separate lid suits a single round candle jar or tin.

Inserts for jars and multi-piece sets

A paperboard or foam insert can be shaped to hold a candle jar or bottle securely inside a magnetic or lid-and-base box, and shaped recesses can separate multiple items in one box. Tell us the jar or tin dimensions and we confirm the insert layout.

Print, finish and materials

Boxes in this range are made in cardboard, corrugated board or coated art paper and can be printed by CMYK offset printing, with finishing options including matt or gloss lamination, varnish, spot UV, foil stamping and embossing/debossing depending on the structure.

Bags and handles

Three paper gift bag styles in this range use cord handles, made from kraft paper — a plain black exterior, a character-illustrated design and a floral-bordered print. Bag size and handle colour are made to your brief.

Sizing, quantity and lead time

Most styles in this range start at 500 pcs, with the hexagonal specialty box from 1,000 pcs. Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and the design service is free.

QUESTIONS

Candle packaging FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom candle boxes?

Most styles in this range start at 500 pcs, with the hexagonal specialty box from 1,000 pcs. Your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the structure and finish you choose.

What's the best structure for a round candle jar or tin?

A paper tube box, with a cylindrical body and a separate lift-off lid, is built for round jars and tins. Magnetic or lid-and-base boxes with a shaped insert suit candles packaged inside a fuller gift set.

Do you have paper bags for candle retail handover?

Yes. Three paper gift bag styles in this range — plain, character-illustrated and floral-bordered — use cord handles and are made from kraft paper, suited to retail handover of a candle purchase.

How long does production take, and do you ship internationally?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help design the candle packaging?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo and artwork references and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

What are these candle boxes used for?

They're used for packaging candle jars and tins, fragrance and candle gift sets, and retail or e-commerce handover for candle brands.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
