BUYING GUIDE

How to plan custom tea and coffee packaging

Choose your structure

Gable-top and picnic-style folding cartons add a built-in carry handle for food gifting, a magnetic gift box with a three-part insert suits a multi-item tea set, mailer boxes suit orders that ship direct to a customer, and a paper tube collection suits loose leaf tea or ground coffee in a cylindrical format.

Material and print

Folding cartons in this range are made in corrugated board or kraft paper; the mailer boxes use coated art paper. CMYK offset printing is confirmed on one mailer box and the paper tube collection; confirm the print method for the other styles with your quote. Finishing options include matt or gloss lamination, varnish and spot UV depending on the structure.

Multi-item tea sets

The divided-insert tea gift box uses a three-part foam or EVA insert to keep several tea items separated and presented cleanly when the lid lifts — tell us the item count and we confirm the compartment layout.

Carrying and handling

The gable-top and picnic-style cartons both include an integrated carry handle, useful for food gifting or takeaway-style presentation.

Sizing, quantity and lead time

MOQ across this range runs from 400 to 1,000 pcs depending on the structure, with the paper tube collection available from 400 pcs. Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and the design service is free.

QUESTIONS

Tea and coffee packaging FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom tea packaging?

The paper tube collection starts from 400 pcs; most other styles in this range start at 500 or 1,000 pcs. Your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the structure you choose.

What's the best structure for loose leaf tea or ground coffee?

A paper tube from the tube collection, with a cylindrical body and coordinated printed designs, suits loose leaf tea and ground coffee. A gable-top or picnic-style folding carton adds a built-in carry handle for food gifting.

Can one box hold several types of tea?

Yes. The divided-insert tea gift box uses a three-part foam or EVA insert to keep multiple tea items separated inside one box.

Do any styles include a built-in carry handle?

Yes. The gable-top and picnic-style folding cartons both have an integrated carry handle for food gifting or takeaway-style presentation.

Do you help design the tea or coffee packaging?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo and artwork references and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

What are these tea and coffee boxes used for?

They're used for packaging loose leaf tea, ground coffee, tea gift sets and food gifting for tea and coffee brands.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
