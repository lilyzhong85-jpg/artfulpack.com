CUSTOM PACKAGING OPTIONS

Make every detail
work together.

Choose the structure first, then coordinate paper, artwork, surface finish and the interior. The final combination is confirmed against your specification and sample.

01 / Start with the opening experience.

Provide internal dimensions where possible. Allow space for inserts, tissue and the product itself; an exterior measurement alone does not define the usable space.

02 / Paper, color and construction.

Match the material to its purpose.

  • Printed surfaces: discuss coated or uncoated papers for your artwork and desired appearance.
  • Natural appearance: consider kraft paper references and how print color appears on that base.
  • Structure: discuss board and corrugated construction according to the box format and intended handling.
  • Color: provide a physical reference or color specification; review the result on the chosen material.

03 / Decide what the customer sees and feels.

Digital & offset printing

Plan the artwork across the exterior and interior. Share print files and identify the panels that need color.

Gold & silver foil

Use metallic accents for a logo or selected details. Confirm the foil area and suitability of fine artwork.

UV effects

Highlight selected areas through surface contrast. Define the placement separately in the artwork.

Emboss & deboss

Add raised or recessed detail. Review the depth and legibility on the selected paper.

Surface lamination

Discuss the desired appearance and feel, including soft-touch options where suitable.

Inserts & lining

Compare paperboard, foam or fabric-style references against the shape and presentation of your product.

These are options presented in the Artful storefront. Suitability and availability depend on the chosen material, construction and artwork.

04 / Complete the set.

Inserts, ribbons and coordinated bags.

  • Send dimensions for every item in a multi-product set.
  • Specify whether the product should lie flat or stand upright.
  • Review ribbon length, handle style and the intended opening sequence.
  • Check the complete pack together before confirming the production specification.
