BUYING GUIDE

How to specify a custom tube box

Choose the construction

Tube boxes are a cylindrical paper tube with a separate end cap or lid that lifts off — a different shape from the rectangular boxes elsewhere on this site. A ribbon can be added on some styles to dress up the closure.

Board and paper

Tubes are made from kraft paper, specialty fine art paper or corrugated board, depending on the strength and finish you need for your product.

Printing and finishing

Spot UV is the most common finish for tube boxes, and matt lamination, varnish and CMYK offset printing are widely available. Embossing/debossing and gloss lamination are options on selected styles, and CMYK plus Pantone spot colour matching keeps your brand colour consistent.

Inserts

A cardboard insert can be added on selected styles to hold your product centred inside the tube.

Size and quantity

Tubes are made to your product's diameter and length. Send your item size, quantity and artwork; MOQ starts at 100 pcs on selected styles, with 500 and 1,000 pcs both common in this range.

QUESTIONS

Tube box FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom tube boxes?

Selected styles start at 100 pcs. Most styles in this range start at 500 or 1,000 pcs, and your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the size and finish you choose.

How does a tube box close?

The tube has a separate end cap or lid that lifts off; a ribbon can be added on some styles for a dressier closure.

How long does production take?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved. Shipping time depends on your destination and shipping method; we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help with the tube design?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo, product dimensions and references, and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

Can you match my brand colour exactly?

Yes, CMYK plus Pantone spot colour matching is available to keep your brand colour consistent across the tube.

What are tube boxes used for?

They're common for cosmetics and skincare products, and general gifts and crafts that suit a cylindrical shape.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
