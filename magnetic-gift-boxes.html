BUYING GUIDE

How to specify a custom magnetic gift box

Choose the construction

Most styles in this range are rigid gift boxes, and many close with a magnetic flap lid. Collapsible versions fold flat for shipping — our standard foldable style packs at about 30 × 20 × 0.6 cm and 0.12 kg per box — which keeps carton volume down until you pack your products. Ribbon-tie and carry-handle versions are also available.

Board and paper

The box body is made from rigid cardboard or greyboard and finished with coated art paper or kraft paper. Corrugated board is available when the set is heavy or has to travel as its own shipping pack.

Printing and finishing

Artwork is printed by CMYK offset printing, with the colour matched to your files. Finish the wrap with matt or gloss lamination or varnish, then highlight the logo with spot UV, foil stamping, embossing or debossing.

Inserts

Hold products in place with a paperboard, foam or EVA, satin-lined or corrugated insert cut to your items — for cosmetics sets, bottles, jewelry and accessories.

Size and quantity

Every box is made to the inner dimensions you need. Send the product size and weight, the quantity and your logo or artwork; your quote confirms the MOQ for the size, board and finish you choose.

QUESTIONS

Magnetic gift box FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom magnetic gift boxes?

Selected styles start at 100 pcs. Most styles in this range start at 500 or 1,000 pcs, and your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the size, board and finish you choose.

Can magnetic gift boxes ship flat?

Yes. Our collapsible styles fold flat for shipping; the standard foldable style packs at about 30 × 20 × 0.6 cm per box and opens into shape when you pack it.

How long does production take?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved. Shipping time depends on your destination and shipping method; we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help with the box design?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo, product dimensions and references, and we prepare the box design for your approval before production.

What inserts can I add?

Paperboard, foam or EVA, satin-lined and corrugated inserts can be cut to fit your products. Tell us how many items go in the box and their sizes.

What are magnetic gift boxes used for?

They are common for apparel accessories, cosmetics and skincare sets, jewelry, and seasonal or corporate gifts.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
