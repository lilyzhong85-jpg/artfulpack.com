BUYING GUIDE

How to specify a custom folding carton

Choose the construction

Folding cartons are flat-pack: the carton is die-cut and printed flat, then erected and closed with glue or tuck flaps when you're ready to pack it. This is a different family from the pre-assembled rigid boxes elsewhere on this site, and it keeps shipping volume down before use.

Board and paper

Cartons are most often made from corrugated board for extra strength, with coated art paper, kraft paper or cardboard used for lighter-duty styles and different finish looks.

Printing and finishing

Artwork is printed by CMYK offset printing and finished with matt or gloss lamination, spot UV and varnish on most styles. Embossing is available on selected styles for extra logo detail.

Inserts

A cardboard or paperboard insert is the most common option for holding products in place; foam inserts are available for products that need extra protection.

Size and quantity

Cartons are made to your product's dimensions. Send your item size, quantity and artwork; MOQ starts at 500 pcs on most styles, with 1,000 pcs also available.

QUESTIONS

Folding carton FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for folding cartons?

Most styles start at 500 pcs, with 1,000 pcs also available. Your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the size, board and finish you choose.

How is a folding carton different from a rigid box?

A folding carton is die-cut and printed flat, then erected and closed with glue or tuck flaps when you pack it — it ships flat rather than pre-assembled like a rigid box.

How long does production take?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved. Shipping time depends on your destination and shipping method; we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help with the carton design?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo, product dimensions and references, and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

Can I add an insert to my folding carton?

Yes. A cardboard or paperboard insert is the most common option; foam inserts are available for products that need extra protection.

What are folding cartons used for?

They're common for food packaging and general gifts and crafts, where flat-pack shipping and on-site assembly help save space.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
