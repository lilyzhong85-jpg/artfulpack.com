BUYING GUIDE

How to plan custom flower boxes

Choose your structure

This range covers floral-printed mailer boxes for orders shipped direct to a customer, a window pouch with a clear display panel, and paper tube boxes with a separate lid, often paired with a candle or small gift inside.

What this packaging is — and isn't

These are gift boxes, mailer boxes and a pouch printed with floral artwork, built as companion packaging for a florist or flower brand — not containers designed to hold cut flowers directly. There's no water-tight lining or floral foam built into any structure in this range.

Print and finish

Printing method varies by structure: one mailer box is printed by CMYK offset printing with matt or gloss lamination finishing available; the other styles in this range — the second mailer box, the window pouch and the paper tubes — are printed without confirmed lamination finishing, so confirm the finish with your quote.

Pairing packaging with a bouquet or candle gift

A floral paper tube can hold a candle as a companion gift alongside a bouquet, while a floral mailer box or window pouch works as the shipped or retail-facing companion piece.

Sizing, quantity and lead time

Most styles in this range start at 500 pcs, with the second mailer box and the window pouch from 1,000 pcs or pairs. Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and the design service is free.

QUESTIONS

Flower packaging FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom flower boxes?

Most styles in this range start at 500 pcs, with some styles from 1,000 pcs or pairs. Your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the structure you choose.

Can these boxes hold fresh cut flowers directly?

No. These are floral-printed mailer boxes, a pouch and paper tubes built as companion packaging for a bouquet — not containers with water-tight lining or floral foam for holding cut flowers directly.

What structure suits a bouquet's companion gift?

A floral mailer box suits a shipped companion gift, a window pouch suits retail display, and a floral paper tube suits a candle or small item packaged alongside the bouquet.

How long does production take, and do you ship internationally?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help design the floral artwork?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo and artwork references and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

What are these flower boxes used for?

They're used as companion packaging for florists and flower brands — mailer boxes, a window pouch and paper tubes sold or presented alongside a bouquet or candle gift, not as flower-holding containers.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
