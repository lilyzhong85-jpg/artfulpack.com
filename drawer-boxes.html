BUYING GUIDE

How to specify a custom drawer box

Choose the construction

Drawer boxes are a two-piece structure: an outer sleeve that holds a sliding inner tray, or drawer. Most styles in this range open by hand with no separate hardware; ribbon pulls, a magnetic closure or a handle are available on selected styles.

Board and paper

The sleeve and drawer are most often made from corrugated board, with cardboard, coated art paper or specialty fine art paper used for the outer wrap and premium finishes.

Printing and finishing

Artwork is printed by CMYK offset printing and finished with matt or gloss lamination and spot UV as standard across this range. Foil stamping and embossing/debossing are common for logo and pattern detail; varnish is available on selected styles.

Inserts

A cardboard insert can be cut to fit your products inside the drawer; tell us your item's size and we'll confirm the insert option for your style.

Size and quantity

Every drawer box is made to your product's dimensions. Send your item size, quantity and artwork; MOQ starts at 100 pcs on selected styles, with 1,000 pcs the most common MOQ in this range.

QUESTIONS

Drawer box FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom drawer boxes?

Selected styles start at 100 pcs. Most styles in this range start at 1,000 pcs, with 500 pcs also available, and your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the size and finish you choose.

How does a drawer box open?

It's a two-piece structure — an outer sleeve holds a sliding inner tray that you pull open by hand. Most styles need no separate hardware; ribbon pulls, a magnetic closure or a handle are available on selected styles.

How long does production take?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved. Shipping time depends on your destination and shipping method; we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help with the drawer box design?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo, product dimensions and references, and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

Can I add an insert to hold my products in the drawer?

Yes, a cardboard insert can be cut to fit your products. Send your item's size and we'll confirm the insert option for your style.

What are drawer boxes used for?

They're common for jewelry packaging, along with food and general gifts and crafts.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
