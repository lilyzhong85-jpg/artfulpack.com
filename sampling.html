SAMPLING & PROJECT PROCESS

Review the details.
Then approve the pack.

Use the sample to answer specific questions about fit, construction and appearance before confirming the production specification.

Choose what you need to check.

Structure & fit

Check usable internal dimensions, clearances, the opening and the way your product sits in the pack.

Artwork & color

Review logo placement, artwork orientation and the intended appearance on the chosen paper.

Finish & assembly

Check closures, wrapped edges, ribbon position and the complete presentation.

Look beyond the closed box.

Review how the box is put together.

  • Check the inside as well as the outer dimensions.
  • Open and close the sample and review the intended packing sequence.
  • Confirm which details the sample represents and which are still to be finalized.
  • Record changes with marked photos and one clear revision list.

Your approval checklist.

01

Identify the version

Confirm the sample reference and artwork revision that you are approving.

02

Resolve the changes

Agree the final dimensions, material, print, finish and insert arrangement.

03

Confirm next steps

Agree sample timing, production schedule, inspection and delivery arrangements with the team.

Samples are free. Sample type, delivery timing and arrangements are confirmed for each project. A visual mockup does not replace a physical fit or material check.
