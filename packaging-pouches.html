BUYING GUIDE

How to specify a custom packaging pouch

Choose the construction

This range covers flexible pouches — stand-up pouches with a standing base, flat-bottom bags, and resealable or window styles, depending on the product line. Tell us which base and closure style fits your product and we'll confirm what's available.

Material and build

Pouches are flexible packaging rather than rigid board, sized to fit your product. Send your item's dimensions and weight so we can confirm the right pouch size and base style.

Printing

The pouch exterior is screen printed, which is the confirmed printing method for this range — not the offset printing and lamination used on the rigid boxes elsewhere on this site. Customized, colour-matched artwork is available so your brand colour prints consistently.

Size range

Pouch sizes vary by product line, from compact pouches to larger stand-up formats. Send us your product's size and weight and we'll confirm the closest pouch size for your order.

Quantity

Send your quantity, artwork and preferred base style; your quote confirms the MOQ for the size you choose. Orders in this range are typically sold in units of 1,000.

QUESTIONS

Packaging pouch FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for packaging pouches?

Orders in this range are typically sold in units of 1,000. Your quote confirms the exact wholesale MOQ for the size and style you choose.

What pouch styles are available?

This range includes stand-up pouches with a standing base, flat-bottom bags, and resealable or window styles depending on the product line. Tell us your product and we'll confirm the closest match.

How long does production take?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved. Shipping time depends on your destination and shipping method; we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help with the pouch design?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo, product dimensions and references, and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

Can you match my brand colour on the pouch?

Yes, customized colour-matched artwork is available so your brand colour prints consistently on the pouch exterior via screen printing.

What are packaging pouches used for?

They're most commonly used for food packaging, and general product packaging where a flexible, lightweight pack is preferred over a rigid box.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
