BUYING GUIDE

How to plan custom christmas packaging

Choose your structure

This range covers four different structures rather than one shape: a hinged specialty box with a ribbon closure, a pillow-shaped folding carton, a mailer box for direct-to-customer shipping, and a paper gift bag for retail handover. Pick the structure that matches how the gift reaches its recipient.

Artwork and print

Festive artwork — penguin and snowflake illustrations, a winter print, a seasonal interior print, and festive printed panels — is applied to each structure's board or paper. Print method and finishing options follow the base structure you choose; your quote confirms what's available.

Sizing and quantity

MOQ starts at 100 pcs on the ribbon-close hinged box, with the mailer box, folding carton and paper bag starting at 1,000 pcs. Your quote confirms the MOQ for the structure and finish you choose.

Timing for peak season

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, the same as any order on this site. Because holiday orders cluster into the same few months, share your target delivery date as early as you can so the production schedule can be planned around it.

Beyond Christmas

The same structures — the hinged box, folding carton, mailer box and paper bag — can be reprinted with Valentine's Day or Halloween artwork instead of holiday motifs, under the same MOQ, lead time and free design service. Tell us the occasion and we confirm the print files needed.

QUESTIONS

Christmas packaging FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom christmas boxes?

The ribbon-close hinged box starts at 100 pcs; the mailer box, folding carton and paper bag start at 1,000 pcs. Your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the structure you choose.

What structures are available for christmas packaging?

This range includes a ribbon-close hinged gift box, a pillow-shaped folding carton, a mailer box and a paper gift bag, each printed with festive artwork.

How long does production take, and when should I order for the holiday season?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved. Holiday orders cluster into the same few months, so share your target delivery date early and we'll confirm a production schedule that fits it.

Do you help with the holiday artwork?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo, artwork references and the holiday theme you want, and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

Can the same box be reprinted for Valentine's Day or Halloween?

Yes. The structures in this range accept different seasonal artwork under the same terms — tell us the occasion and we confirm the print files needed.

What are these christmas boxes used for?

They're used for seasonal gifting, food and snack presentation, and e-commerce shipping of gifted items around the holidays.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
