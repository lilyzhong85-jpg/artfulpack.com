BUYING GUIDE

How to plan custom wine packaging

Choose your bottle structure

A magnetic gift box with fitted bottle compartments holds a bottle securely, and a mailer box or hinged specialty box with three fitted recesses holds a set of bottles inside one box. A tall cylindrical paper tube with a separate cap suits a single small-format bottle. A ribbon-tie folding gift box, confirmed for wine gifting, offers a general presentation format for a bottle or gift set without a fitted recess.

Structural fit for wine and spirits

Some of the bottle-recess and cylindrical structures in this range were originally built for fragrance and small bottle gifting — the single-bottle recess and the tall cylindrical tube with a cap are the same shape a wine or spirits bottle needs, so they suit wine gifting structurally even where the original product was designed around a fragrance bottle.

Print, finish and inserts

Boxes are made in rigid greyboard or coated art paper and printed by CMYK offset printing, with finishing options including matt or gloss lamination, varnish, spot UV and foil stamping. A paperboard insert can be fitted to hold bottles securely in their recesses.

Coordinating a multi-bottle set

Three-bottle recess boxes and bottle gift mailers can carry a colour-block lid or a patterned interior, with slot sizing built to your bottle dimensions so a set of bottles sits securely as one gift.

Sizing, quantity and lead time

MOQ across this range runs from 500 to 1,100 pcs depending on the structure. Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and the design service is free.
