BUYING GUIDE

How to specify a custom paper gift bag

Choose the construction

These are paper gift bags, not rigid boxes — there's no board structure inside. Most styles pair a carry handle with a ribbon trim; handle-only and ribbon-only versions are also available. Some styles are sold as sets that match a box in the same collection.

Handles and trims

The handle is the main hardware decision: handles are paired with a ribbon trim on most styles in this range. Tell us the handle style you want and we'll confirm what's available for your bag size.

Paper and printing

Bags are made from coated art paper, kraft paper or specialty fine art paper, and your artwork is printed on the bag exterior. Finish and paper type vary by style, so confirm the paper with your quote.

Size and shape

Bag sizes are made to fit your products, from small gift bags to larger carrier sizes. Send us the item you're packing and its dimensions so we can confirm the right bag size.

Quantity

Send your quantity, artwork and preferred handle style; your quote confirms the MOQ for the paper and size you choose. Selected styles start at 100 pcs, with 500 pcs the most common MOQ in this range.

QUESTIONS

Paper gift bag FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom paper gift bags?

Selected styles start at 100 pcs. Most styles in this range start at 500 pcs, with 1,000 pcs also available, and your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the paper and size you choose.

What handle styles are available for paper gift bags?

Most styles in this range pair a handle with a ribbon trim; handle-only and ribbon-only versions are also available. Tell us your preferred style and we'll confirm what fits your bag size.

How long does production take?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved. Shipping time depends on your destination and shipping method; we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help with the bag design?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo, product dimensions and references, and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

Can I match my gift bags to a box in the same collection?

Some styles in this range are sold as matching sets with a box, in seasonal and botanical print styles. Tell us the box you're pairing with and we'll confirm a matching bag option.

What are paper gift bags used for?

They're common for fashion accessories and general gifts and crafts, and can be paired with a matching box for a complete gift set.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
