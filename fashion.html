BUYING GUIDE

How to plan custom apparel packaging

Choose your structure

Paper gift bags are the largest group in this range, suited to retail handover, alongside hinged specialty boxes and magnetic gift boxes for boxed apparel presentation, mailer boxes for orders that ship direct to a customer, and drawer boxes for compact accessories. Pick the structure based on how the garment reaches the customer — over the counter, boxed as a gift, or shipped direct.

Custom shoe boxes

Footwear is a notable use case in this range: several styles are ordered with an MOQ set in pairs rather than pieces, sized to hold a pair of shoes. The same hinged, lid-and-base and mailer structures used for apparel apply to custom shoe boxes, with the size built to your pair dimensions.

Print, finish and inserts

Artwork is printed by CMYK offset printing, with finishing options including matt or gloss lamination, varnish, spot UV, foil stamping and embossing/debossing depending on the structure. Paperboard, foam, satin-lined or corrugated inserts can be added to hold folded garments or accessories in place.

Sizing for garments and accessories

Bag and box sizes are built to your product dimensions, whether that's a folded shirt, a boxed accessory set or a pair of shoes. Handle style, ribbon detail and logo placement on bags are made to your brief.

Quantity and lead time

Most styles in this range start at 500 pcs, with some styles available from 100 pcs and shoe-box styles counted in pairs. Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and the design service is free.

QUESTIONS

Custom apparel packaging FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom apparel boxes?

Selected styles start at 100 pcs. Most styles in this range start at 500 pcs, and shoe-box styles are quoted by the pair — your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the structure you choose.

Do you make custom shoe boxes?

Yes. Several styles in this range are ordered with an MOQ set in pairs and sized to hold a pair of shoes, using the same hinged, lid-and-base or mailer structures as the rest of the range.

How long does production take, and do you ship internationally?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help design the apparel packaging?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo and artwork references and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

Can I get matching bags, boxes and mailers for one collection?

Yes. Paper gift bags, hinged boxes, magnetic gift boxes and mailer boxes in this range can share the same colour and logo finish for a coordinated collection.

What are these apparel boxes used for?

They're used for retail handover, boxed gift presentation, e-commerce shipping and footwear packaging for clothing and accessory brands.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
