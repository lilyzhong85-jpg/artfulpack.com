Custom Die-Cut Handle Gift Box with Swirl Print

A gift box in specialty fine art paper with an integrated die-cut carry opening and a swirl pattern printed across the exterior. It closes with a ribbon tie, prints by CMYK offset printing, and can be finished with matt or gloss lamination, varnish, spot UV or embossing/debossing; a paperboard insert can be added. It suits gifting and cosmetics presentation. Swirl artwork, colour and finish are made to your brief, with orders from 1,000 pcs.

QUESTIONS

Die-Cut Handle Gift Box with Swirl Print FAQ

What is the MOQ for the die-cut handle gift box with swirl print?

The MOQ for this style is 1,000 pcs. Share your target quantity and we quote the price tiers above it.

Which parts can be customized?

Size, board, printing and finish are made to your brief. Finishing options for this style include matt lamination, gloss lamination, varnish, spot UV, embossing / debossing. You can also add a paperboard insert.

Is the handle a separate attachment?

No, the carry opening is die-cut directly into the box structure rather than a separate attached handle, so it's part of the same printed panel as the swirl artwork.

How long do production and delivery take?

Production typically takes 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved. Delivery time depends on your destination and shipping method; we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help with the design?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo, product dimensions and references, and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
