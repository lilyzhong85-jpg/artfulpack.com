Frequently asked questions

Does the FSC reference apply to every product?

The reference alone does not establish a claim for an individual product. Ask us to confirm the material, supply route and documentation for your chosen packaging.

Can I add an FSC label to my packaging?

Include this requirement in your brief. Label eligibility and artwork approval need to be confirmed for the specific order before printing.

How do I request the current certificate?

Contact us with licence FSC-C190651 and the packaging you are considering. Request the current certificate and scope documents for review alongside the official register.

What quality or testing documents should I request?

Share your destination market, product use and acceptance criteria. We can discuss the available documentation and any additional inspection or testing needed for your specification.
