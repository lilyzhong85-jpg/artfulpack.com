BUYING GUIDE

How to plan custom electronics packaging

Choose your structure

This range covers three structures: a folding carton with a wide printed front panel for cable packaging, a two-piece lid-and-base box with a phone-shaped fitted tray, and a hinged box with a shaped insert for small electronic accessories. Pick the structure based on the item's shape and how it's presented.

Fitted inserts for protection

A phone-shaped tray or a shaped insert cut for small electronic accessories keeps the item from shifting inside the box, with room for printed instructions alongside the parts in the hinged box style.

Print, finish and materials

All three styles are made in coated art paper or corrugated board and printed by CMYK offset printing, with finishing options including matt or gloss lamination, varnish and spot UV.

What this range doesn't include

This range doesn't include a hang-tab retail display carton — the three structures here are a cable carton, a two-piece phone box and a hinged accessory box, each suited to shipping or gift presentation rather than peg-hook retail display.

Sizing, quantity and lead time

The cable carton and phone tray box start at 1,000 pcs; the hinged electronics box starts at 500 pcs. Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and the design service is free.
