DONGGUAN JINGQIAO PACKAGING

Meet the company
behind ArtfulPack.

From a product brief
to a packaging specification.

ArtfulPack presents the packaging range of Dongguan Jingqiao Packaging Products Co., Ltd., also shown as Jingqiao Packaging on the Artful Alibaba storefront.

The company introduction describes a practical sequence: analyze the product dimensions and customer requirements, develop a sample, then move into production after the sample or artwork is approved.

The range includes paper gift boxes, folding boxes, drawers, paper tubes, shopping bags, corrugated cartons and packaging pouches, with a focus here on beauty, jewelry and fashion brands.

THE PRODUCTION SEQUENCE

From artwork approval
to finished packaging.

Artwork and sample confirmation set the direction for production. The storefront illustrates the following sequence, with printing and finishing stages selected for each specification.

  1. Artwork design
  2. Sample confirmation
  3. Plate making
  4. Material cutting
  5. Printing
  6. In-process QC check
  7. Film lamination
  8. UV coating
  9. Foil hot stamping
  10. Embossing & debossing
  11. Die cutting & creasing
  12. Gluing & folding
  13. Final QA inspection
  14. Bundling & delivery

Not every finish applies to every product; the agreed specification determines the production route.
