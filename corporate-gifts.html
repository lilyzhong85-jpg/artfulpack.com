BUYING GUIDE

How to plan custom corporate gift boxes

This page is organized by buyer, not by structure

There's no single "corporate gift box" shape. The styles here are general-purpose collection or size-collection boxes — magnetic gift boxes, mailer boxes, a lid-and-base box and a hinged gift set box — chosen because they're offered across multiple sizes or a coordinated colour run, which suits a company ordering one consistent gift across many recipients.

Pick a size run for consistent branding

Several styles are sold as a size collection or colour collection rather than a single fixed size, so a company can order the same design across small, medium and large gift tiers, or across a colour run, and still present one consistent branded look.

Print, finish and inserts

Boxes are made in cardboard, corrugated board or specialty fine art paper and can be printed by CMYK offset printing, with finishing options including matt or gloss lamination, varnish, spot UV and embossing/debossing depending on the structure. A paperboard insert can be added for the divided-insert gift set box.

Choosing between formats

A magnetic gift box suits an in-person handover, a mailer box suits gifts shipped direct to employees or clients, and the divided-insert gift set box suits a multi-item gift. Compartment layouts are available on selected styles.

Sizing, quantity and lead time

Most styles in this range start at 500 pcs, with the divided-insert gift set box from 1,000 pcs. Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and the design service is free.
