BUYING GUIDE

How to plan custom pet packaging boxes

Choose your structure

This range covers two stand-up pouches with a self-standing base, confirmed for pet food packaging, and a rectangular folding carton with a folding bottom flap, confirmed for pet product packaging. Pick the pouch for a shelf-standing fill format or the carton for a boxed retail format.

Pouch features

Standing-base pouches hold their shape on a shelf or counter without support; options across this range include a die-cut carry hole or an integrated carry handle. Pouch dimensions are set to match your fill volume.

Print, finish and materials

Artwork prints across the front panel of the pouch or carton. The folding carton is made in corrugated board and printed by CMYK offset printing, with finishing options including matt or gloss lamination, varnish or spot UV, and paperboard or foam/EVA inserts available.

Every style confirmed for pet use

All three styles in this range are confirmed for pet food or pet product packaging use in the source catalogue, rather than a generic structure borrowed from another category.

Sizing, quantity and lead time

All three styles in this range start at 1,000 pcs. Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, and the design service is free.
