BUYING GUIDE

How to specify a custom mailer box

Choose the closure

This range is corrugated shipping and mailer cartons, not rigid boxes with a separate lid. Most styles close with a self-locking tuck-front flap, a front locking tab, or a four-flap cube closure that folds and locks at the point of use. A small number of styles add a magnetic closure, handle or ribbon.

Board and paper

The carton body is corrugated board for shipping strength, wrapped or printed directly with coated art paper; kraft paper and specialty fine art paper are available on selected styles.

Printing and finishing

Artwork is printed by CMYK offset printing and finished with matt or gloss lamination and varnish on most styles. Spot UV is common; foil stamping and embossing/debossing are available on selected styles.

Inserts

Cardboard or paperboard inserts are the most common way to hold products in place; foam inserts are available for products that need extra protection in transit.

Size and quantity

Packed sizes vary by product — send your item's dimensions, weight and quantity, and we confirm the box size, board and MOQ in your quote. Wholesale MOQ starts at 500 pcs on most styles, with bulk orders quoted per size and board.

QUESTIONS

Mailer box FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom mailer boxes?

Most styles start at a wholesale MOQ of 500 pcs, with 1,000 pcs also common depending on size and board. Your quote confirms the MOQ for the size and finish you choose.

Do mailer boxes have a separate lid like a rigid gift box?

No. Mailer boxes are corrugated cartons that close with a self-locking tuck-front flap, a front locking tab, or a four-flap cube closure — there's no separate lid to fit or lose.

How long does production take?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved. Shipping time depends on your destination and shipping method; we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help with the mailer box design?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo, product dimensions and references, and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

Can I add an insert to protect my products in transit?

Yes. Cardboard or paperboard inserts are the most common; foam inserts are available for products that need extra cushioning.

What are mailer boxes used for?

They're common as custom shipping boxes for e-commerce orders of fashion accessories, gifts and crafts, and food products, where the box needs to double as both packaging and the shipping carton.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
