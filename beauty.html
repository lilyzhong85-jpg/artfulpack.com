BUYING GUIDE

How to plan custom cosmetic packaging

Choose your structure

Cosmetic packaging in this range is built across several structures rather than one dedicated "cosmetic box" shape: hinged and specialty gift boxes are the largest group, alongside paper tube boxes, magnetic gift boxes, mailer boxes, lid-and-base boxes and drawer-style boxes. Pick the structure that matches your product — a hinged box for a boxed skincare set, a paper tube for a cylindrical jar or bottle, a drawer box for compact accessories — then apply your branding to it.

Print, finish and inserts

Artwork is printed by CMYK offset printing on the board or paper base, with finishing options including matt or gloss lamination, varnish, spot UV, foil stamping and embossing/debossing depending on the structure you choose. Paperboard, foam, satin-lined or corrugated inserts can be added to hold jars, bottles or multi-piece sets securely in place.

Match structure to product type

Paper tube boxes suit round jars and bottles with a lift-off or separate lid, drawer boxes suit compact accessory sets that slide out for presentation, and magnetic or hinged boxes suit boxed skincare and makeup sets that need a full lid closure. Mailer boxes are built for orders that ship direct to a customer rather than boxed retail handover.

Sizing and quantity

Most styles in this range start at 500 or 1,000 pcs, with selected styles available from 100 pcs. Your quote confirms the MOQ for the specific structure, size and finish you choose.

Lead time and design service

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved, the same as any order on this site. The design service is free — send your logo and artwork references and we prepare the design for your approval before production.
