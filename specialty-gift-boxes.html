BUYING GUIDE

How to specify a custom specialty gift box

Choose the shape and construction

This range covers rigid gift boxes beyond the standard magnetic-flap style — hinged lids, book-style and clamshell boxes, faceted or polygon shapes, and house-shaped seasonal boxes. Most styles close with a ribbon tie, and magnetic closures and carry handles are also available on selected styles.

Board and paper

Boxes are made from corrugated board, cardboard or specialty fine art paper, finished with coated art paper for the outer wrap. Corrugated board suits heavier sets, while fine art paper gives a premium finish for retail gifting.

Printing and finishing

Artwork is printed by CMYK offset printing and finished with matt or gloss lamination and varnish as standard across this range. Add spot UV, foil stamping, or embossing and debossing to highlight your logo.

Inserts

Most boxes in this range use a cardboard or paperboard insert cut to your products; foam inserts are available for fragile items, with cotton, satin-lined, aluminium foil-lined or kraft corrugated inserts on selected styles.

Size and quantity

Every box is made to your product's size. Send your dimensions, quantity and artwork; your quote confirms the MOQ for the shape, board and finish you choose — selected styles start at 100 pcs, most start at 500 or 1,000.

QUESTIONS

Specialty gift box FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for specialty gift boxes?

Selected styles start at 100 pcs. Most styles in this range start at 500 or 1,000 pcs, and your quote confirms the wholesale MOQ for the shape, board and finish you choose.

What shapes are available for specialty gift boxes?

This range covers rigid box shapes beyond the standard magnetic-flap style, including hinged lids, book-style and clamshell boxes, faceted or polygon shapes, and house-shaped seasonal boxes.

How long does production take?

Lead time is typically 15 days after the artwork and sample are approved. Shipping time depends on your destination and shipping method; we ship to more than 50 countries.

Do you help with the box design?

Yes, the design service is free. Send your logo, product dimensions and references, and we prepare the design for your approval before production.

Can I add an insert for my products?

Yes. Cardboard or paperboard inserts are the most common; foam inserts are available for fragile items, and cotton, satin-lined, aluminium foil-lined or kraft corrugated inserts can be added on selected styles.

What are specialty gift boxes used for?

They're common for cosmetics and skincare sets, gifts and crafts, seasonal or corporate gifting, and fashion accessories.

Are samples free?

Yes, samples are free. Share your sample requirements in the inquiry and we confirm the sample brief and delivery timing with your quote.
